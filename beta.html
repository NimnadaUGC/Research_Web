What Beta Participation Involves

The study design has separate routes for a controlled application comparison and formative skill use.

Controlled tasks

The draft protocol compares manual work, generic AI and framework use through BA Mate on comparable fictional requirements cases. Exact instructions and releases must be frozen before measured sessions.

Practical feedback

Practitioners can report usability, friction and workflow experience. Skill feedback is analysed separately from the primary application comparison.

Participant information

Selected testers receive the relevant instructions, consent information and feedback route directly. Sharing company material requires appropriate permission; public issue reports should use sanitised examples.

Results pending: Public availability of the skill does not mean the research study is complete or that the framework has a demonstrated improvement percentage.
