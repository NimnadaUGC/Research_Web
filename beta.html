Planned BA Toolkit Outputs

Elicitation Assistant

Guides iterative requirement elicitation by generating targeted clarification questions to reduce ambiguity in user stories and requirement statements.

Ethics and Bias Checker

Flags bias-sensitive language, hidden assumptions, and stakeholder fairness issues to improve transparency and ethical quality of BA artifacts.

Documentation Generator

Converts reviewed outputs into structured BA documents, including refined user stories, acceptance criteria, and traceable requirement notes.

Expected Research-Driven Outcomes

Quality Improvements

  • Clearer and more complete requirements through adaptive prompting loops
  • More transparent BA artifacts with explicit assumptions and rationale
  • Lower bias risk with structured ethics checkpoints

Workflow Impact

  • Faster BA documentation cycles in Agile settings
  • Better BA-developer alignment during handover
  • Preserved BA control via human approval at every critical step
