What the Research Studies

Business Analysts work with incomplete, changing and sometimes conflicting stakeholder information. This research specifies how AI can assist that work while people remain responsible for business meaning, evidence checks and approval.

Ground work in evidence

Keep source material, decisions, assumptions and unresolved questions visible as requirements are drafted and refined.

Keep human decisions explicit

AI can propose questions, drafts and checks. Practitioners select or correct them; an authorised person approves identified artifact revisions.

Manage change faithfully

Trace requirements and documents to their sources and revisions so changed evidence can trigger review, revalidation and a new decision.

Research status: F4-draft.1 is a proposed method. Testing, practitioner feedback and analysis are being advanced; no comparative effectiveness result is reported here yet.

Core lifecycle: Scope → evidence → clarification → requirements and criteria → process model → validation → document → approval → change. Stages may be revisited; change occurs when needed. Developer handover is an optional export.

Evaluation Now Under Preparation

The study connects the design to observable requirements work and practitioner experience.

Controlled comparison

The draft protocol defines A: manual work, B: generic AI, and C: framework use through BA Mate on comparable fictional cases. Requirements quality and process measures will be examined after the study is frozen and run.

Realistic beta use

Practitioners will report workflow friction, usefulness, failures and adaptations. Consent and any company-content permissions are separate from installing or using the software.

Skill usability

The public skill is a separate portability and formative stream. Its observations will not be silently pooled with the application-based A/B/C comparison.

Results pending: Participant outcomes, effect estimates and final framework refinements will be published only after evidence collection, scoring and analysis.
