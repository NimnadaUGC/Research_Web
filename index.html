Research Overview

Business Analysts in the SDLC

BAs act as critical intermediaries translating stakeholder needs into clear, testable requirements across Agile and traditional SDLC environments. This project augments (does not replace) BAs with AI in Sri Lankan SME contexts.

Core Innovation

  • Adaptive prompt engineering for iterative requirements elicitation and refinement
  • Ethical prompting and bias-reduction framework for transparency
  • Hybrid AI agents (human-in-the-loop) supporting Agile BA workflows

Problem Definition

1. Ambiguity in Requirements

Elicited requirements often contain vague terms or missing details; generic AI fails to ask targeted clarification questions.

2. Ethical Risk and Low Transparency

AI-generated artifacts may embed bias in wording and prioritization; teams lack trust without explicit assumptions and explanations.

3. Alignment and Handover Friction

SME Agile teams face communication gaps between stakeholders, BAs, and developers, leading to rework and delays.

Research Questions

Main RQ: How can AI-enhanced methodologies and hybrid agents improve BA work while ensuring ethical safeguards and preserving the BA's human role?

  • SQ1 (Elicitation): How well do adaptive prompt workflows reduce ambiguity and improve clarity and completeness?
  • SQ2 (Ethics): Which ethical prompting and bias checks best improve transparency and reduce bias in BA artifacts?
  • SQ3 (Hybrid workflow): How much does a human-in-the-loop hybrid agent prototype improve BA efficiency and BA-developer alignment in Agile SMEs?

Research Objectives

1

Adaptive Prompt Methodologies

Enhance requirements elicitation and refinement (target: 20-40% improvement via A/B rubric evaluation).

2

Ethical Prompting and Bias Checks

Improve transparency and reduce bias (target: 15-30% reduction via checklist + bias-lint metrics).

3

Hybrid AI Agents Prototype

Support BA workflows in Agile SMEs (target: 30-50% efficiency and alignment gain with human-in-the-loop).

Methodology (Design Science + Empirical)

1

Adaptive Prompting

Stakeholder input -> Draft -> Ambiguity detection -> Clarification questions -> Refined user stories and SRS.

2

Ethics and Bias Layer

Bias-sensitive language detection, stakeholder fairness prompts, transparency notes (assumptions and traceability).

3

Hybrid Agent Toolkit

Elicitation Assistant + Ethics Checker + Documentation Generator + Human feedback loop (Streamlit-ready prototype).
