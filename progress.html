Data Collection Sources

  • Primary: Anonymized SME BA interviews & real SDLC artifacts (15–25 participants, 2–3 SME organizations)
  • Backup: Public benchmarks & synthetic datasets (GitHub issues, Jira specs, OpenStack requirements)
  • Ethics: Consent forms, anonymization protocol (SOP drafted, ethics approval pending)

Access: Subject to ethical approval. Anonymized dataset to be archived in university research repository post-publication (12-month embargo for participant privacy).

Quantitative Metrics

  • Clarity Score (1–5 rubric): Completeness, specificity, testability (based on IEEE 29148 standard)
  • Bias Flag Count: # of detected risks per artifact (bias-lint + manual audits)
  • Time to Completion: Baseline manual workflow vs. AI-assisted (in hours)
  • Defect Rate: Post-development rework incidents (caused by vague/incorrect specs)
  • Transparency Score: Presence of explicit assumptions, trade-offs, traceability notes

Qualitative Evidence

  • BA Interviews: Usability (System Usability Scale), trust, cognitive load, adoption barriers
  • Developer Feedback: Likert-scale survey on spec clarity, completeness, handover friction
  • Stakeholder Satisfaction: Fairness perception, representation in final artifact
  • Thematic Analysis: Open-ended responses coded by 2+ independent reviewers (Cohen's kappa ≥ 0.70)

Target Success Criteria

  • Clarity: 20–40% improvement post-intervention vs. baseline
  • Bias: 15–30% reduction in flag counts + improved transparency scores
  • Efficiency: 30–50% time savings in specification phase
  • Adoption: Positive feedback from 80%+ of pilot BAs (Likert ≥ 3.5/5)
